Embodying the Revolution

The Hebrew Experience and the Globalization of Modern Sports in Interwar Palestine

Rutgers University Press, 2025

Embodying the Revolution offers a new reading of Zionism by situating Jewish sport in interwar Palestine within the broader history of modern athletics. Drawing on extensive archival sources and informed by contemporary literary and historical theory, the book highlights an unexpected Zionist unease with modern competitive sports.

It reveals a society in which athletes did not automatically emerge as symbols of national pride, thereby shedding new light on both the appeal of sport and conventional interpretations of the “New Jew” as a purely top-down ideological construct.

Zionism

Emotions, Language and Experience

Cambridge University Press, 2024

Focusing on the visceral and embodied historical aspects of the linguistic modernization of Hebrew, I argue that cultural studies of Zionism have reached an impasse, which the history of emotions could help us overcome.

Interpreting Zionist texts not solely as symbolic myths but as a historical, lived experience, this book advocates for the significance of the history of emotions and experience as an innovative methodology with profound ethical implications for our polarized era.
